## 📖 About DuoWidget

**DuoWidget** is designed exclusively for couples, best friends, or two people who want a quiet, distraction-free connection. 

Unlike noisy messaging apps or social networks filled with feeds and group chats, DuoWidget focuses on a single intimate connection:
Whenever you send a hand-drawn doodle, a camera snap, or a quick heartfelt note, it appears **instantly right on your partner's Android home screen widget** like a digital polaroid.

## 🧩 Adding the Widget to Your Home Screen

To see your partner's moments update live without even opening the app:

1. Go to your phone's **Home Screen**.
2. **Press and hold (long-press)** any empty space on your home screen.
3. Tap **Widgets** from the pop-up menu.
4. Scroll down and find **DuoWidget**.
5. Drag and drop the widget onto your home screen.
6. *(Optional)* Long-press the widget to resize it to your preferred dimensions (2x2, 3x3, or 4x4).

## 📖 How to Use (Step-by-Step Guide)

### Step 1: Create an Account
1. Open **DuoWidget**.
2. Enter your **Name/Nickname**, **Email**, and a secure **Password**.
3. Tap **Create Account & Pair 🌿** (or sign in if you already have an account).

---

### Step 2: Connect with Your Partner
After logging in, you will be on the **Pairing Screen**:
* **If you are the FIRST to set it up**:
  1. You will see your **Unique Pairing Code** (e.g. `DUO-HBH5`).
  2. Tap **Copy** and send this code to your partner via WhatsApp, SMS, or Telegram.
  3. Wait on this screen — as soon as your partner enters your code, your screen will automatically transition to the Home screen!
* **If your partner already sent you their code**:
  1. Enter or paste their code into the **"Enter Partner's Pairing Code"** box.
  2. Tap **Connect with Partner 💞**.
  3. Both phones are now securely paired!

---

### Step 3: Send Your First Moment
On the Home Screen, use the bottom dock to send moments:
* ✏️ **Doodle Button**: Open the drawing sheet to sketch and tap **Send to Partner**.
* 💌 **Note Button**: Type a quick thought, select a sweet preset, and tap **Send Note**.
* 📷 **Photo Button**: Snap a quick camera picture or pick a photo from your gallery.

Within seconds, your partner's home screen widget will update to display your new moment!

---

### ⚙️ Managing Your Connection
Tap your **Profile Name** at the top right of the Home Screen to open the settings menu:
* **Change Display Name**: Tap the edit pencil next to your name.
* **Copy Pairing Code**: Copy your unique code anytime.
* **Change Partner / Reconnect**: Switch partners if either of you ever changes devices or creates a new account.
* **Disconnect & Unpair 💔**: Smoothly disconnect from your current partner.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

We believe intimate couple spaces should be completely private:

1. **True End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)**:
   - When two partners pair, an AES-256 cryptographic key is generated and stored exclusively on your two devices.
   - Text, sketches, and photos are encrypted *before* they leave your phone.
2. **Zero Server Retention**:
   - The cloud server only acts as a temporary delivery relay.
   - The exact moment your partner's phone receives the update, the server permanently purges the message from existence.
3. **Local Storage Only**:
   - Your photos, notes, and drawings live exclusively on your phone's private edge storage.

## 💡 Troubleshooting & FAQ

#### Q: The note says "No internet connection"
* Make sure your Wi-Fi or Mobile Data is turned on and active.
* If you have a custom VPN or Private DNS (like AdGuard) active on your device, set it to Automatic or disable it temporarily.

#### Q: The widget isn't updating immediately
* Ensure Android's **Battery Optimization** is set to "Unrestricted" or "Optimized" for DuoWidget so Android doesn't put background updates to sleep.
* Tapping the widget once will force-refresh your partner's latest moment.
